  Negative modals express different degrees of restriction, from <Highlight color="yellow">simple inability</Highlight> to <Highlight color="green">strong warnings</Highlight>.
</RuleBox>

### Degrees of Restriction

| Modal | Meaning / Function |
|---|---|
| **can't** | Impossibility (-) OR Ability (-) |
| **don't / doesn't have to** | Necessity (-) — *It is not necessary.* |
| **shouldn't** | Advice (-) |
| **had better not** | Strong Advice (-) |
