## Purpose

Used to express hypothetical or unreal situations about the present.

### The "Be" Verb Rule

Always use **were** for the subjunctive, never was — regardless of the subject (I, he, she, it).

### Tense Shifts Cheat Sheet

| Fact / Present | Shift for Wish |
|---|---|
| is / am / are | **were** |
| can | **could** |
| will | **would** |
| have | **had** |

### Key Vocabulary

- **Could** = Ability / Possibility (Want to, but can't)
- **Would** = Desired action (Want a situation to change)
