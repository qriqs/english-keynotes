<RuleBox title="Rule of Thumb">
  The choice between Said and Told depends entirely on whether you include the <Highlight color="yellow">listener</Highlight> in the sentence.
</RuleBox>

### 1. Using "Told"
Needs a person/object pronoun immediately after it.

<Example>
  Roberto <strong>told</strong> <Highlight color="yellow">me</Highlight> he played soccer every day.
</Example>

### 2. Using "Said"
Is more flexible. No direct person is needed after the verb.

<Example>
  Maria <strong>said</strong> that she had a modern car.
</Example>
