## Why use it?

Use the passive voice when the agent (person doing action) is:
- Understood
- Unknown
- Unimportant

### Tense Formations

| Tense | Form of the "Be" Verb |
|---|---|
| Simple Present | am / is / are |
| Simple Past | was / were |
| Present Cont. | is being / are being |
| Present Perfect | has been / have been |
| Future | will be |
