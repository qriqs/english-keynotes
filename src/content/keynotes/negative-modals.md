## Degrees of Restriction

| Modal | Meaning / Function |
|---|---|
| **can't** | Impossibility (-) OR Ability (-) |
| **don't / doesn't have to** | Necessity (-) — It is not necessary. |
| **shouldn't** | Advice (-) |
| **had better not** | Strong Advice (-) |

### Key Vocabulary

- **Squander** = to waste money in an extravagant way.
