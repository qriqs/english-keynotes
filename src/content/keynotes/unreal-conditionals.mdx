<RuleBox title="The Golden Rule">
  Just like wish statements, you must use <Highlight color="yellow">were</Highlight> for ALL subjects in the "if" clause to imagine a different present reality.
</RuleBox>

### 1. The "If" Clause (The Condition)

Formula: **If** + Subject + <Highlight color="yellow">were / past verb</Highlight>

<Example>
  If I <Highlight color="yellow">were</Highlight> the president...
</Example>

### 2. The Result Clause

Formula: Subject + <Highlight color="green">would</Highlight> + base verb

<Example>
  ...I <Highlight color="green">would</Highlight> lower taxes immediately.
</Example>
