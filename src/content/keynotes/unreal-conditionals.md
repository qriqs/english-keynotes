## Usage

Used to imagine a different present reality.

> Just like wish statements, you must use **were** for ALL subjects in the "if" clause.

### 1. The "If" Clause (The Condition)

**If** + Subject + **were / past verb**

Ex: If Joe Biden were healthy...

### 2. The Result Clause

Subject + **would** + base verb

Ex: ...he would still be the president.
