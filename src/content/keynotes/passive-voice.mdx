<RuleBox title="When to use it (Context)">
  Use the passive voice when the agent (person doing the action) is <Highlight color="yellow">understood</Highlight>, <Highlight color="yellow">unknown</Highlight>, or <Highlight color="yellow">unimportant</Highlight>.
</RuleBox>

### 1. The Master Formula

Subject + **To Be (in matching tense)** + <Highlight color="green">Verb (Past Participle)</Highlight>

### 2. Tense Formations

| Tense | Form of the "Be" Verb | Example (The car...) |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple Present** | am / is / are | is <Highlight color="green">washed</Highlight> every day. |
| **Simple Past** | was / were | was <Highlight color="green">washed</Highlight> yesterday. |
| **Present Cont.** | is being / are being | is being <Highlight color="green">washed</Highlight> right now. |
| **Present Perfect** | has been / have been | has been <Highlight color="green">washed</Highlight> many times. |
| **Future** | will be | will be <Highlight color="green">washed</Highlight> tomorrow. |
