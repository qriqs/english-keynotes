## Pronoun Mapping

Used to connect ideas and provide more information about a noun.

- **who / that** → People
- **that / which** → Things / Animals

### Key Vocabulary

- **Endangered** = animal going to extinct
- **Wild** = living natural
- **Awareness** = state of perceiving
