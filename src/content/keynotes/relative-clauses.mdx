<RuleBox title="Purpose">
  Relative clauses are used to connect ideas and provide <Highlight color="yellow">more information</Highlight> about a noun without starting a new sentence.
</RuleBox>

### Pronoun Mapping

| Pronoun | Used For |
|---|---|
| **who / that** | <Highlight color="yellow">People</Highlight> |
| **that / which** | <Highlight color="green">Things & Animals</Highlight> |
| **where** | Places |
| **when** | Times / Dates |

### Examples in Context

<Example title="People">
  The scientist <strong>who</strong> studies <Highlight color="yellow">endangered</Highlight> species is giving a lecture today.
</Example>

<Example title="Animals">
  The tiger is a <Highlight color="green">wild</Highlight> animal <strong>which</strong> requires vast territories.
</Example>
