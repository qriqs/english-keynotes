## Rule of Thumb

The choice depends entirely on whether you include the **listener** in the sentence.

### Told

Needs a person immediately after it.

> "Roberto **told** me he played soccer every day."

### Said

Is more flexible (no direct person needed).

> "Maria **said** that she had a modern car."
